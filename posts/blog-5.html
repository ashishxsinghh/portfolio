My Photography Journey: From a 7th Grader’s Curiosity to a Passion for Capturing Moments

My love for photography started in Class 7th with a simple mobile phone. I was fascinated by how moments could be captured and transformed into stories. With my phone always in hand, I experimented with different styles—portraits, landscapes, and candid shots—getting creative with lighting, angles, and composition.

However, everything changed on October 28th, 2023, during my time in Class 11th (high school), when I got my first iPhone. This was a game-changer. The advanced camera technology allowed me to take my photography skills to the next level. Suddenly, I could capture even more detailed and vibrant shots, pushing the boundaries of mobile photography. I felt like I had unlocked a new level of creativity, and my passion grew stronger.

Not only did my photography improve, but I also ventured into cinematography. With the iPhone, I started experimenting with video, capturing stories in motion. I worked hard to make the most of this opportunity, trying my best to master both photography and cinematography, elevating my content to a professional level.

Cinematography continues to be an outlet where I express my creativity, always seeking to improve and capture the world in unique ways.
